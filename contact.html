Get in Touch

Have a question about one of our SEO guides or free tools? Found a possible error, or would you like to suggest a topic? You can contact SEO Growth Hub by email.

Email: randeorout@gmail.com

What You Can Contact Us About

  • Content corrections: report an inaccurate explanation, outdated step or broken reference in one of our guides.
  • Topic suggestions: recommend a beginner-friendly SEO subject or a practical tool you would find useful.
  • Website feedback: tell us about navigation, readability, mobile display or accessibility problems.
  • Resource questions: ask for clarification about an educational article or a browser-based tool.
  • Project enquiries: share a website URL and a short description of the SEO support you are looking for.

How to Report a Correction

To help us review a possible error, include the exact page URL, the heading or sentence you are referring to, what you believe should be corrected and a reliable source that supports the change. For technical SEO topics, official documentation is especially helpful.

What to Include in a Project Enquiry

If your message is about a website, include its public URL, your target audience or country, the pages you want reviewed and the main question you want SEO to help answer. Do not send account passwords or private access credentials by email.

Response Expectations

Messages are reviewed as time allows. A message does not create an ongoing support agreement, and we cannot guarantee a response time or a particular SEO outcome.
