How to use the brief well

  1. Validate the topic. Use keyword research and Google Search Console to understand real queries and search intent.
  2. Study the questions, not just the rankings. Review search results and community questions to understand what readers still need.
  3. Add first-hand value. Include your own process, screenshots, calculations, examples or tested templates. Do not invent case studies.
  4. Write for completeness. Answer the main question early, then cover the useful details without padding the article to an arbitrary word count.
  5. Connect the topic. Link to related pages such as the on-page SEO guide and technical SEO guide.
  6. Review after publishing. Use Search Console to monitor impressions, clicks, CTR and queries after the page has been crawled.
