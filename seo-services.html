Practical SEO Support for Growing Websites

SEO Growth Hub publishes educational resources and offers scoped, practical support for website owners who need help understanding and improving their search visibility. The right scope depends on your website, goals, current content and available data.

1. Keyword Research and Content Planning

Identify the questions your audience searches for, group related terms by intent and map topics to the pages that should answer them. A useful research brief can include:

  • Primary topic and closely related phrases.
  • Search intent and the type of page that best answers it.
  • Competitor pages to review for content gaps.
  • A recommended outline and internal links to related resources.

2. On-Page SEO Review

Review important page elements so they accurately describe the content and help visitors navigate the site. This can include:

  • Page titles and meta descriptions.
  • One clear main heading and descriptive subheadings.
  • Useful, original content that answers the reader's question.
  • Descriptive URLs, image alternatives and contextual internal links.

3. Technical SEO Audit

Check whether important pages can be discovered, crawled and indexed, and whether common technical issues create friction for visitors. A review may cover:

  • HTTP status codes, broken internal links and redirect paths.
  • XML sitemap and robots.txt configuration.
  • Canonical URLs and index directives.
  • Mobile usability, image delivery and Core Web Vitals.

4. Content Improvement Recommendations

Compare the purpose of a page with the questions it is meant to answer. Recommendations focus on missing explanations, practical examples, accurate supporting sources, clearer structure and relevant links rather than adding words simply to increase length.

What We Need to Review a Website

To discuss a possible project, share the website URL, the pages you care about, your target audience and country, and the main outcome you want from organic search. Access to Google Search Console can help distinguish pages that are receiving impressions from pages that need more visibility.
